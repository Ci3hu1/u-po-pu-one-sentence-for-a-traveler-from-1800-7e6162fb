# Strategy — One Sentence for a Traveler from 1800 (v1)

## The recipe (own, not copied)

One place, one image: **a post office**. A traveler from 1800 already knows
what a post office is — letters, ink, shelves of books — so the whole modern
internet is translated into that one room: every letter in the world arrives
the moment the ink dries (instant global messaging), and every book ever
printed waits on its shelves for any hand that asks (all human knowledge,
open to anyone). Two facts of the internet, one venue, one sentence.

## Why this should win the judge's criteria, in order

1. **Explains the internet** — it says what the internet *does* (moves
   everyone's words instantly; puts every book within reach), not what it is
   called. A reader from 1800 walks away knowing the thing.
2. **Builds from 1800 knowledge** — post office, letter, ink, book, shelves,
   hand. Zero post-1800 coinages (enforced by a test with a forbidden-word
   list of 90+ modern terms).
3. **True, taken literally** — letters do arrive as soon as they are written;
   the world's books are, in effect, on open shelves for anyone. No claim
   about wires, machines, or magic.
4. **Every word earns its place** — 162 of 200 characters. No list of
   features, no filler adjectives, one clause per half of the idea.
5. **Memorable phrase** — "the moment the ink dries": the instant a letter
   is finished, it is already there. That is the image a reader keeps.

## Tried and dropped

- **Market-square / town-crier variant** — "every town crier in the world
  shouts at once". Dropped: the internet is not shouting, and a square full
  of criers reads as noise, not as a tool. Less true (criterion 3).
- **Library-only variant** — "a library where every letter lands the moment
  it is written". Dropped: a library explains the knowledge half but not the
  messaging half; a post office naturally holds both letters *and* books.
- **Two-metaphor version** — post office *plus* a separate library clause.
  Dropped: two venues reads as a list (criterion 4 penalises crammed lists);
  folding the books into the post office's shelves keeps one image.

## Measured edge

- 162 characters (limit 200), exactly 1 sentence, ASCII only.
- `main.py` is bare-stdlib Python 3.13, deterministic, byte-identical across
  runs, writes `/output/entry.txt` mode 0644.
- `tests/test_entry.py` covers length, single-sentence shape, ASCII,
  no-modern-words, output file bytes/mode, and determinism.
